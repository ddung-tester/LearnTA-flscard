# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Vietnamese students and self-learners studying English vocabulary. They sit down for short, focused sessions (10–20 minutes) — at a desk or on a phone — motivated but easily pulled away. Primary job: quickly add words they've encountered, then practice recalling them through flashcards and quizzes. They want to feel progress without friction.

## Product Purpose

An English vocabulary learning app for creating word decks, adding English–Vietnamese card pairs, studying with flip-card flashcards, and testing with auto-generated multiple-choice quizzes. Built as a learning project (React, Node.js, MySQL), but designed to be immediately usable by any Vietnamese student or self-learner. Success: a user can create a deck, add cards, and finish a quiz in under five minutes with zero confusion.

MVP scope: no login or authentication until the core flow is stable — create deck, add cards, study flashcards, take quiz. Auth is a post-MVP concern. The Home page, if present, should feel like an entry point into the app, not a marketing landing page.

## Brand Personality

Clear, friendly, focused. The interface should feel like a well-organized notebook — approachable but not childish, modern but not corporate. Encourages without rewarding compulsively. Makes studying feel achievable, not heavy.

## Anti-references

- Generic Quizlet blue (too familiar, no identity of its own)
- Enterprise dashboards (dense, intimidating, data-heavy)
- Heavy gamification (streaks, badges, excessive popups)
- Too many animations or decorative effects
- Dense, cluttered layouts that are hard to scan

## Design Principles

1. **Reduce friction first.** Every screen should answer: what does the user do next? Remove anything that competes with that answer.
2. **Earn the next interaction.** Don't show everything at once. Surface controls when they're relevant to where the user is in the workflow.
3. **Warmth without whimsy.** The personality is friendly, not playful. Avoid cartoon metaphors, excess illustration, and gamified rewards.
4. **Clarity over completeness.** Show one thing at a time on learning screens. Resist adding stats and indicators until they serve the task.
5. **Consistency as trust.** Same patterns, spacing, and language everywhere. Predictability lowers cognitive load for repeat sessions.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA. Reduced-motion support throughout. Text legible at standard sizes for non-native English readers. All interactive elements keyboard-accessible and comfortable to use for 10–20 minute study sessions.
